Project case study

MessMate: a mess meal tracking platform

A mobile-first PWA that digitizes daily meal tracking, billing and payment reminders for a mess business with 50+ real customers.

JavaScriptFirebaseFirestorePWANetlify
Live Demo View Code

The problem

A mess / tiffin business serving 50+ real customers tracked daily meals in a paper notebook, which led to errors in balances and payments. MessMate replaces it with a fast, mobile-first app.

Features

  • Real-time meal, balance and payment tracking
  • Read-only, token-authenticated customer view
  • Offline-first behavior with Firestore persistence and a service worker
  • Bilingual English / Hindi interface
  • Usage-based billing with variable meal plans and carry-forward balances
  • Automated payment reminders

Engineering decisions

  • Atomic batched transactions keep each customer's meal balance, payment records and public view consistent under concurrent updates.
  • Custom Firestore Security Rules restrict all writes to one authenticated owner account, while customers get a read-only view.
  • PWA architecture so it installs on a phone and works with poor connectivity.
